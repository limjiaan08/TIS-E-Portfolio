**Design Thinking**\n
By completing this design thinking project, I have gained significant experience in integrating and applying a user-centered approach to system design. This was achieved by understanding the real challenges faced by UTM students such as long waiting queues, unavailability of appointment booking and the difficulties in accessing their previous visits or healthcare records at PKU. It is undeniable that I have improved my teamworking skills, communication and leadership after accomplishing this project with my group members. However, if we were given more time to prepare this project, we could enhance it by communicating with actual PKU staff and carry out more usability testing with UTM students for refinement on system's interface and practicality. Overall, this valuable experience has improved my confidence in critical thinking, problem solving and communication. I believe these essential skills will furher improve myself during my academic journey and future career.
