**Industry Talk 3: Clarity Techworks Sdn Bhd**

As part of the SECP1513 – Technology and Information System course, my group and I prepared an academic poster based on an industry talk by Clarity Techworks Sdn Bhd, a fintech company operating in Singapore and Malaysia.

Through this session, we gained valuable insights into how ICT plays a critical role in the fintech and blockchain industry. The speakers shared real-world industry experiences, including organizational structure, technology stack, job roles, and skills required for various ICT positions such as Web Developer, Android Developer, UI/UX Designer, and QA Analyst.

This talk helped me understand that a successful ICT career requires not only technical skills, but also communication, analytical thinking, creativity, and continuous learning. Overall, the session motivated me to better prepare myself for future ICT careers and to contribute meaningfully to digital transformation and innovation.
