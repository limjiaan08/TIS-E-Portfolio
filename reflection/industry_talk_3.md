**Industry Talk 3: Clarity Techworks Sdn Bhd**

As part of the SECP1513 – Technology and Information System course, my group and I prepared an academic poster based on an industry talk by Clarity Techworks Sdn Bhd, a fintech company operating in Singapore and Malaysia.

Through this session, we gained valuable insights into how ICT plays a crucial role in the fintech and blockchain industry. The speakers shared real-world experiences related to organizational structure, technology stack, job roles, and skills required for various ICT positions such as Web Developer, Android Developer, UI/UX Designer, and QA Analyst.

The session could be further improved by including more live demonstrations or case studies, allowing students to better visualize how fintech systems are developed and deployed in real projects.

This talk helped me understand that an ICT career requires not only strong technical skills, but also communication, analytical thinking, creativity, and continuous learning to solve real-world problems effectively.
