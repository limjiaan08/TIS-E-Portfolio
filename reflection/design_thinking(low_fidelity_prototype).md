Through this project, I gained practical experience in applying the Design Thinking methodology to solve real-world problems. I learned how to understand user needs through surveys, define clear problem statements, and transform ideas into a digital prototype using Figma. This project also strengthened my teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills, as close collaboration was required throughout each phase.

One area for improvement is involving more users during the testing phase to obtain broader and more diverse feedback. With additional time, the prototype could also be enhanced with higher-fidelity designs and more detailed interactions to better reflect a real-world system.

Overall, this project helped me realize the importance of empathy and user-centered design, especially in healthcare systems. It showed that well-designed digital solutions can significantly improve user experience and system efficiency.
