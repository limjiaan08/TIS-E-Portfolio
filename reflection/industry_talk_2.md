On 18 December 2025, I attended an Industry Talk on Project Management and System Development by Ts. Hj. Abdul Alim Bin Abdul Muttalib, Head of Technology and Innovation at Senurai Commerce Sdn Bhd. He shared his professional journey after graduating from UTM nearly 10 years ago, along with his experience working on various system development projects.
Through this session, I learned that system development goes beyond coding, as it involves planning, analysis, design, testing, and maintenance. The speaker also emphasized the importance of project management methodologies, such as Waterfall and Agile, in managing software projects effectively.

The session could be further improved by including more real-world project case studies or demonstrations, which would help students better visualize how these methodologies are applied in actual industry environments.

Overall, this talk provided valuable insights into industry practices and the essential skills required to succeed in the computer science and software development field.
