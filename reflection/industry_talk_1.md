On 20 November 2025, I attended an online Industry Talk by PPG as part of the SECP1513 – Technology Information and System course. The session introduced how paints and coatings are applied across various industries and highlighted that coatings are not only for appearance, but also for protection, safety, durability, and sustainability.

The speaker shared how innovation and research play a crucial role in enhancing product performance while minimizing environmental impact. This gave me a clearer understanding of how engineering and technology contribute to sustainable industrial solutions.

Since the talk was conducted online, the experience could be improved by incorporating more interactive elements, such as live demonstrations or virtual tours, to further increase student engagement.
Overall, this talk broadened my awareness of how technology is applied in real industrial environments and introduced potential career opportunities in materials, chemical, and manufacturing-related industries.
